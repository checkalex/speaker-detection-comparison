01

Identical audio, isolated detection

Both models received the same 16 kHz mono WAV for each clip. This compares Precision-3 with Nemotron 3 Diarization, not the full Nemotron 3.5 Transcribe pipeline.

02

Human names, subtitle-based times

Speaker identities were filled by a human in Checksub subtitle exports. Speech boundaries were not independently annotated. Display padding and missing intervals can inflate timing errors. Only the Dave chorus has explicit overlap reference labels.

03

Strict, consistent scoring

Zero boundary collar; overlap included; full-clip evaluation. Speaker IDs are matched to names globally and one-to-one, so splitting or merging identities is penalized.

04

A small, specialized sample

Three English animation clips, with music and exaggerated voices. Dave exceeds NVIDIA’s eight-speaker capacity. These results do not establish performance on customer recordings, other languages, or recordings with matched speaker budgets.

05

Speed and cost need a separate test

Precision-3 used a hosted API; NVIDIA used local GPU inference. Queueing, upload and model load have different scopes. This report makes no apples-to-apples speed or cost claim.

05CONCLUSION

Keep Precision-3
as the baseline, for now.

There is no broad quality winner established yet. Precision-3 leads across all three clips, but the two recordings within NVIDIA’s speaker limit are nearly tied.

Retain the current baseline while running a larger, independently timed comparison. NVIDIA’s local deployment and competitive results within its capacity justify that next test. Neither system recovers all five chorus voices here.

PROVISIONAL RECOMMENDATION

BEFORE A REPLACEMENT DECISION

1

Tighten the reference

Annotate actual speech boundaries and all overlaps, independently of subtitle display timing.

2

Broaden and match the test set

Add customer-style interviews in our working languages; compare recordings within the eight-speaker limit, with larger casts assessed separately.

3

Measure the complete trade-off

Compare correction time, deployment cost and latency under consistent conditions, then inspect the final Checksub editor output.
